ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS (SELECTED)

Net sales for fiscal 2025 were $402.3 billion, an increase of 6% compared
to fiscal 2024. Services net sales grew 13% year over year and reached an
all-time high. Products net sales increased 3%.

Gross margin was 46.8% for fiscal 2025, compared to 45.6% in fiscal 2024,
driven by a higher mix of Services revenue.

Operating income was $127.8 billion. The Company's effective tax rate was
16.2%.

SELECTED BALANCE SHEET DATA (as of fiscal year end)
- Cash and marketable securities: $61.5 billion
- Total debt: $96.7 billion
- Shareholders' equity: $66.8 billion

RISK FACTORS (SELECTED)
- The Company is subject to global regulatory scrutiny including antitrust
  and digital-markets regulation in the EU.
- A material portion of manufacturing is concentrated with a small number
  of suppliers and geographies.
- Foreign-exchange volatility may affect reported results.
